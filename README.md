# AgentDesk

> **Turn every enquiry into an opportunity.**

AgentDesk is an AI-powered customer and revenue operations platform that helps businesses capture leads, answer customer questions, qualify prospects, automate follow-ups, book appointments, and hand important conversations to the right human.

The goal is simple: **fewer missed leads, faster responses, less repetitive work, and a clearer path from enquiry to conversion.**

---

## What AgentDesk Does

AgentDesk is designed around a complete business workflow rather than a standalone chatbot:

**Capture → Understand → Respond → Qualify → Act → Follow Up → Escalate → Measure**

### 1. Capture

Bring incoming customer conversations and enquiries into a structured workflow.

- Website enquiries
- AI website conversations
- Voice/customer calls where configured
- Missed-call recovery
- Lead capture
- Contact and conversation records

### 2. Understand

The agent identifies what the customer is trying to accomplish and collects only the information required for the next step.

Examples:

- Product or service enquiry
- Sales lead
- Support request
- Appointment request
- Callback request
- Existing customer request

### 3. Respond

AgentDesk uses the business's approved information and knowledge to provide relevant answers.

The agent should not invent:

- Prices
- Availability
- Policies
- Guarantees
- Business facts
- Actions it has not actually completed

When the required information is unavailable or confidence is insufficient, the workflow should ask for clarification or escalate.

### 4. Qualify

For sales workflows, AgentDesk can collect the information a business needs to determine the next action.

For example:

- Requirement
- Budget
- Location
- Timeline
- Service/product preference
- Contact information
- Other business-specific qualification criteria

The objective is not to ask as many questions as possible. It is to collect the **minimum useful information** needed to move the lead forward.

### 5. Act

Depending on configuration and permissions, AgentDesk can trigger business actions such as:

- Create or update a lead
- Assign a lead
- Notify a salesperson
- Schedule an appointment
- Request a callback
- Update a customer record
- Trigger a follow-up
- Create a support workflow
- Hand a conversation to a human

### 6. Follow Up

AgentDesk helps prevent qualified opportunities from disappearing after the first interaction.

Follow-ups should be:

- Configurable
- Timed
- Context-aware
- Limited
- Automatically stopped when the customer responds or the workflow reaches its end condition

### 7. Human Handoff

Automation should stop when a human is needed.

Escalation can occur when:

- The customer asks for a person
- The request is outside the agent's scope
- The agent lacks reliable information
- The customer needs special attention
- A sensitive or high-impact action requires approval
- A business rule requires human intervention

A handoff should include the conversation context, customer details, qualification information, actions already taken, unresolved issue, and recommended next step so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.

### 8. Measure

AgentDesk should make the operational impact visible.

Businesses can track metrics such as:

- Conversations
- Leads captured
- Qualified leads
- Response time
- Appointments
- Follow-ups
- Human handoffs
- Resolution rate
- Workflow/action failures
- Conversion or outcome data where available

## Who AgentDesk Is For

AgentDesk is particularly useful for businesses where:

- A high volume of enquiries arrives every day
- Speed of response affects conversion
- Salespeople spend time answering repetitive questions
- Leads are manually followed up
- Appointments or callbacks are common
- Important enquiries can be missed outside business hours
- Managers need visibility into what happens to incoming leads

Strong use cases include:

- Real estate and property sales
- Education and coaching
- Clinics and appointment-based businesses
- D2C and e-commerce
- Travel businesses
- Recruitment
- Home and professional services
- Marketing agencies managing lead-generation workflows

The strongest initial use case is a **high-ticket business with significant inbound lead volume and a small or medium sales/support team**.

## Example: Real Estate Lead Workflow

A prospect sends:

> "I'm looking for a 3BHK in Noida. My budget is around ₹2 crore."

AgentDesk can:

1. Respond immediately.
2. Understand the requirement.
3. Ask relevant qualification questions.
4. Capture the customer's details.
5. Provide approved project/business information.
6. Determine whether the lead meets configured criteria.
7. Offer a callback or site visit.
8. Create/update the lead record.
9. Notify the salesperson.
10. Start an appropriate follow-up sequence.
11. Stop automation when the customer responds or a human takes ownership.
12. Preserve the complete conversation context for the salesperson.

The salesperson receives a **qualified opportunity**, not an unstructured message.

## Core Agent Types

AgentDesk can support purpose-built agents for different business outcomes.

| Agent | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales / Lead Agent** | Capture, qualify and route inbound opportunities |
| **Support Agent** | Resolve repetitive customer questions and requests |
| **Appointment Agent** | Schedule, reschedule and confirm appointments |
| **Follow-Up Agent** | Nurture opportunities and prevent lead leakage |
| **Knowledge Agent** | Answer business-specific questions using approved knowledge |
| **Operations Agent** | Execute configured repetitive internal workflows |

These agents should share the same underlying customer context, permissions, business knowledge, action system and audit history.

## Lead Lifecycle

AgentDesk should treat leads and customer requests as structured records, not only conversations.

**New → Engaged → Qualified → Action Pending → Booked/Converted → Follow-Up → Human Review → Closed**

The exact states can vary by workflow, but every important enquiry should have a clear status and owner.

## Product Principles

### Outcome over novelty

AgentDesk exists to improve a business workflow. A feature should have a clear user, workflow and outcome.

### Automation with control

Businesses decide what the agent can access and what actions it can perform.

### Human when needed

Human handoff is a core capability, not an error state.

### Accuracy over guessing

The agent should use approved information and acknowledge uncertainty.

### Context follows the customer

A human should receive the information already collected by the agent.

### Every important action should be traceable

Businesses should be able to understand what happened and why.

### Measure business impact

The platform should connect automation activity to useful operational outcomes.

### Reusable across industries

The underlying engine should remain consistent while templates and workflows adapt to each business.

## The Value Proposition

AgentDesk should be presented in business language.

**Without AgentDesk:**

Enquiry → manual response → delayed follow-up → possible missed opportunity

**With AgentDesk:**

Enquiry → immediate response → qualification → action → human handoff when needed → follow-up → measurable outcome

The core value is:

- Faster response
- Fewer missed enquiries
- More consistent follow-up
- Less repetitive work
- Better lead qualification
- Faster human handoff
- Better customer experience
- Greater operational visibility

AgentDesk should not promise a specific revenue increase unless the customer's own data supports that claim.

## Implementation Direction

AgentDesk should be built as a controlled AI workforce layer rather than a generic chatbot.

The platform should consistently connect:

**Channels**
→ **Conversation**
→ **AI reasoning**
→ **Business knowledge**
→ **Rules & permissions**
→ **Actions**
→ **CRM/customer records**
→ **Human handoff**
→ **Follow-up**
→ **Analytics**

Every new feature should fit into this architecture and preserve the core workflow.

---

## Builder Acceptance Criteria

Before considering a major feature complete, verify:

- Does it solve a real business problem?
- Does it fit the Capture → Understand → Respond → Qualify → Act → Follow Up → Escalate → Measure workflow?
- Does it preserve existing customer context?
- Are permissions and business rules respected?
- Can failures be detected?
- Can the business see what happened?
- Is human handoff available where appropriate?
- Does the feature avoid unnecessary complexity?
- Does it work reliably in a production workflow?
